SENIOR-FIRST BY DESIGN

Verification should not become another obstacle.

Older adults can encounter extra friction when phone, account, or identity records do not match cleanly. Shared or family mobile plans can be one source of confusion.

That does not necessarily mean verification is impossible. A good experience should explain what is happening and guide the consumer to the identity provider's supported recovery or assisted-verification path.

BEFORE YOU START

Phone-number check

  1. Use your own personal mobile number when asked.
  2. If the system cannot confirm it, do not keep retrying blindly.
  3. Follow the identity provider's instructions for another verification method or assisted verification.
  4. Have your photo ID and requested supporting documents ready.

EnrollHere.me cannot bypass or override an identity provider's verification process.
