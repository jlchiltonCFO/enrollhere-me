Clear answers

Questions are part of learning.

Is EnrollHere.me part of ID.me?

No. EnrollHere.me is an independent EnrollHere educational resource. ID.me is a separate company. Its name appears only to identify the service covered by this training.

Can I create my real account here?

No. This website has no credential or document forms. When you are ready, begin on the official website for the agency or organization you need and follow its ID.me link.

Will this training ask for my password or identity documents?

No. Never enter an ID.me password, security code, Social Security number, selfie, or identity document into EnrollHere.me.

What if I already have an ID.me account?

Use the account you already have. Avoid creating a duplicate. ID.me’s official help center can help if you cannot remember how to sign in.
