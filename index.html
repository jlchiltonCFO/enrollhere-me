COMMON QUESTIONS

Good questions deserve clear answers.

Is EnrollHere.me part of ID.me?

No. EnrollHere.me is an independent educational project from JLC Limited. ID.me is a separate company. Its name appears only to identify the service covered by this training.

Will this training ask for my ID.me password?

No. Never enter an ID.me password, security code, Social Security number, or ID photo into EnrollHere.me. Real credentials belong only on ID.me’s website.

Do I need to create a new ID.me account?

If you already have an ID.me account, use it. Avoid creating a duplicate account. If you are unsure, use ID.me’s official account help.

Will everyone need to verify their identity?

No. Requirements depend on the service you are trying to use and the status of your ID.me account. Follow the instructions shown by ID.me and the service you started from.
