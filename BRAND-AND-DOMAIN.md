# Brand and domain guidance

EnrollHere.me should feel trustworthy without suggesting that it is owned, operated, or endorsed by ID.me or a government agency.

## Public addresses

Use `https://enrollhere.me` for the public go-to-market site and `https://id.enrollhere.me` for the Lovable training application. The go-to-market call to action should lead to the training application after that subdomain is connected and tested.

Both addresses must remain controlled by JLC Limited. The page header and footer should identify JLC Limited, and the independent-training disclosure should remain visible near the first mention of ID.me. The `id` subdomain describes the training topic; it does not mean the site is operated or endorsed by ID.me.

Do not use a domain that could be mistaken for an ID.me or government address. Avoid names containing `idme`, `.gov`, or words such as `official`, `authorized`, or `partner` unless written authorization supports the claim.

## ID.me references

Use `ID.me` in text to identify the subject of the training. If an ID.me logo is added, use only an approved asset from ID.me's current brand guidelines, preserve its clear space and minimum size, and keep EnrollHere.me and JLC Limited branding at least equally prominent.

## Veteran-owned business statement

The current badge says “Veteran-owned small business.” Do not add “SBA-certified,” a government seal, or a certification mark unless JLC Limited has current documentation authorizing that exact claim and asset.

## Launch checklist

- Confirm `enrollhere.me` and its `id` subdomain are owned and controlled by JLC Limited.
- Point `id.enrollhere.me` to the Lovable project using Lovable's current custom-domain instructions.
- Enable HTTPS and redirect all HTTP traffic to HTTPS.
- Change the go-to-market training buttons to `https://id.enrollhere.me/training` only after the custom domain is serving the correct app.
- Keep the independent-training disclosure on every page.
- Recheck all links and legal notices after connecting the domain.
- Run the accessibility checks in `ACCESSIBILITY-CHECKLIST.md` on the public address.
